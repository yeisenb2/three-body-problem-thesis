# Restricted Planar Three-Body Problem, Convexity, and Global Surface of Section

**Yael Eisenberg** · M.A. Thesis in Mathematics · Katz School of Science and Health, Yeshiva University · July 2020
**Advisor:** Professor Marian Gidea

📄 [Read the thesis (PDF)](MA_Thesis.pdf)

## Abstract

The restricted planar three-body problem has many applications in celestial mechanics. This thesis studies
the problem for the Sun, Earth, and a spacecraft. It derives the locations of the L1 and L2 Lagrange points,
regularizes the system with the Levi-Civita transformation, and analyzes the Hessian of the resulting
Hamiltonian. Using the Sylvester criterion, it examines where the energy surface is strictly convex, which
determines the energy levels at which a disk-like global surface of section is guaranteed to exist.
